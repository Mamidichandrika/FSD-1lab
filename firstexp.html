Elon Musk

Elon Reeve Musk was born on June 28 in 1971 is a businessman.
He is known for his leadership of Tesla, SpaceX, X (formerly Twitter),
and the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE). Musk has been
the wealthiest person in the world since 2021; as of May 2025,
Forbes estimates his net worth to be US$424.7 billion.
