Intresting Facts about Technology

Quirky Innovations

  • Sony’s AIBO robot dog, released in 1999, was an AI-powered pet that could bark, wag its tail, and play with a ball without the need for traditional pet care
  • The first webcam was set up to monitor a coffee pot at the University of Cambridge so people could see if coffee was available.

Technology in Culture

  • The word “Google” was actually a spelling mistake: it was meant to be “Googol” (which means 1 followed by 100 zeros), but the misspelled domain was available and stuck
  • The earliest email was sent in 1971 by Ray Tomlinson, setting a precedent for digital communication.

Surprising Tech Origins

  • The first computer mouse, invented in 1964 by Douglas Engelbart, was made of wood and was rectangular in shape
  • The original Macintosh computer included the engraved signatures of the entire development team on its inside case.
