Nano Banana

Introduction

Nano Banana is the popular codename for Google DeepMind’s advanced image editing and generation model. Officially released in August 2025 as Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, it quickly became a viral trend under the nickname “Nano Banana.”

What is Nano Banana

Nano Banana is an AI image editing / generation model developed by Google DeepMind, part of the Gemini line.

Popular Use / Trends

  • The model has become popular as a social media trend: people transforming selfies or photos into toy-like 3D figurines, designer pet toys, fantasy avatars, etc.
  • Many published prompt ideas and “viral prompts” have circulated: e.g. “1/7 scale realistic figurine on acrylic base”, “anime style figurine”, “superhero figure”, etc.
  • It’s being used through Gemini app / Google AI Studio / related platforms.

Why It Matters / Impact

  • Makes powerful image editing more accessible to non-experts: you don’t need deep graphics skills; you can use simple prompts.
  • Drives creative trends / social engagement: people like trying new filters, sharing imaginative edits, etc. This kind of tool shapes what people do on social media.
  • Pushes forward what AI image editing tools are expected to do: better consistency, more realistic blending, natural prompt understanding.
